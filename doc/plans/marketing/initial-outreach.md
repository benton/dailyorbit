# Initial Outreach Plan

## Launch Day (2026-04-07)

### 1. LinkedIn Post

Post with a live link to dailyorbit.org. Draft:

> I built an AI-powered satellite communications intelligence tool. It searches every FCC IBFS filing ever made, generates daily AI newspaper editions, and lets you interrogate the data via RAG chat. Try it: dailyorbit.org

This alone demonstrates more than most portfolios. ~30 minutes of work, reaches the right audience organically.

### 2. Targeted Outreach to SatCom Legal Counsel

The handful of law firms that dominate FCC filings (Hogan Lovells, Milbank, etc.) would immediately understand the value proposition — they bill hundreds per hour for manual IBFS research.

Short email template:

> Subject: Tool that makes IBFS research 10x faster
>
> I built a tool that indexes every FCC IBFS/ICFS filing, generates AI summaries, and provides RAG-powered search across the entire corpus. Live demo at dailyorbit.org — no login required.
>
> If your team does satellite or international telecom regulatory work, I think you'll find it useful. I'm also available to discuss custom development.
>
> Benton Roberts
> benton@bentonroberts.com | bentonroberts.com

### 3. FCC/Space Industry Community Posts

Places where people who care about IBFS filings actually hang out:
- Reddit r/satellitecomm
- Space Policy Online
- SatNews
- Via Satellite forums

Adapt the LinkedIn post for each audience — more technical detail for space policy, more product focus for industry.

## Week 1-2: Medium Effort

### 4. Short Demo Video (2-3 min)

Walk through: search a filing, browse the newspaper, chat with a consultant. Post on LinkedIn, embed on the `/about` page. Video converts better than text for non-technical audiences.

### 5. "How I Built This" Blog Post

Technical-audience version: architecture decisions, AI integration patterns (multi-model inference, RAG pipeline, automated newspaper generation), DuckDB as the universal data layer, what worked and what didn't. Post on personal blog, cross-post to Hacker News.

## Not Worth Doing Now

- SEO optimization, paid ads, social media presence — overkill for a job-hunting demo
- Email newsletter — the newspaper feature already demonstrates this conceptually
- Product Hunt / similar launch platforms — wrong audience for a niche regulatory tool
